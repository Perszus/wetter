# Screenshots

Both stores read this directory, so a screenshot added here appears in both
listings.

Requirements, which are Play's and are stricter than F-Droid's:

- PNG or JPEG, no alpha channel
- between 320 px and 3840 px on each side
- at least two, at most eight
- name them so they sort in the order you want them shown: `1.png`, `2.png`, …

Play additionally needs, uploaded through the console rather than from here:

- a 512×512 PNG app icon, no alpha, no rounded corners of your own
- a 1024×500 feature graphic

None of these exist yet. Taking screenshots of a weather app before it can draw
a precipitation timeline would mean shipping a listing that shows the one thing
the app does not do — see docs/RELEASING.md.
