WHY THIS EXISTS

Wetter is meant to be installable by anyone, from wherever they already get
software. For many people that is Google Play, and Play's Developer Distribution
Agreement places conditions on redistribution that section 6 of the GPL can be
read as forbidding. The copyright holder may distribute their own work on any
terms they choose, so this changes nothing while there is one author — but it
would become a real obstacle the moment somebody else contributes, and it is
much easier to grant now than to collect from a dozen contributors later.

The permission is deliberately narrow. It removes an obstacle to distribution and
nothing else: the source stays copyleft, modifications stay copyleft, and nobody
gains the right to ship a closed fork.

Note that Wetter on F-Droid and Wetter on Google Play cannot share a signature —
Play holds the signing key for anything distributed through it. Install from one
or the other.
